Sheikh Md. Tahmid Ullah

Research Associate & Lab Manager (dBme Lab) · Teaching Assistant, Department of Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology, University of Chittagong

Microbial genomics and molecular epidemiology researcher with an M.Sc. (Thesis) and B.Sc. (Honours) in Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology (both 3.73 / 4.00) from the University of Chittagong. Serving as Research Associate and Lab Manager at the Disease Biology and Molecular Epidemiology (dBme) Research Laboratory, my work bridges wet-lab microbiology and computational genomics across antimicrobial resistance (AMR), coastal and gut metagenomics, 16S rRNA profiling, viral and bacterial whole-genome sequencing, and international research collaborations including with the University of Oxford.

  • Metagenomics & 16S rRNA
